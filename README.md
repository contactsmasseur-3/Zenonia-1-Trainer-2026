## 🎮 About the Game

ZENONIA 1 is a single-player action RPG originally developed and published by Gamevil. It first released in South Korea on August 28, 2008, reached iOS in North America on May 24, 2009, and was restored for Steam by Com2uS Holdings and CFK on August 31, 2026. The game combines real-time combat, character progression, exploration, quests, equipment management, and moral choices.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **ZENONIA 1**. This trainer adjusts core RPG resources and progression systems in ZENONIA 1, including health, SP, gold, experience, hunger, equipment durability, inventory weight, attributes, and skill points.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to reduce grinding, experiment with Warrior, Paladin, or Assassin builds, explore the story more freely, or replay the campaign with customized resource and combat settings.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents Regret's HP from decreasing during exploration and combat.
- **Infinite SP** — Keeps the SP gauge full for repeated use of class skills and abilities.
- **No Hunger** — Stops hunger from decreasing while traveling, questing, and fighting.
- **Unlimited Gold** — Provides unlimited gold for purchasing equipment, potions, food, repairs, and other items.
- **Experience Multiplier** — Increases experience gained from defeating monsters and completing quests.
- **Edit Experience** — Allows direct adjustment of the character's experience total for faster leveling.
- **Unlimited Stat Points** — Provides extra attribute points for customizing the character's build.
- **Unlimited Skill Points** — Provides additional points for unlocking and upgrading class skills.
- **Infinite Item Durability** — Prevents equipped weapons and armor from losing durability.
- **Ignore Inventory Weight** — Removes weight restrictions so more equipment, food, and consumables can be carried.
- **Unlimited Consumables** — Prevents selected potions, food, and SP-restoring items from being consumed.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **God Mode** — Blocks incoming damage while preserving normal movement and attack controls.
- **One-Hit Kills** — Causes regular enemies to be defeated with a single successful attack.
- **Damage Multiplier** — Raises outgoing damage to speed up fights against monsters and bosses.
- **Movement Speed** — Adjusts Regret's movement speed for faster travel and more responsive positioning.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement option after loading a save and returning to normal gameplay. Adjust speed gradually, especially in narrow dungeon areas, to avoid overshooting entrances, objects, or quest locations.

### Tools & Inventory

Gold, weight, durability, and consumable options affect ZENONIA 1's equipment and resource systems. Activate unlimited weight before collecting large amounts of loot, and use durability protection before extended dungeon runs. Avoid changing item values while buying or selling.

### World & Environment

ZENONIA 1 follows Regret through towns, fields, caves, quests, and story events shaped by good and evil choices. Resource options are useful for exploration, hunger management, repairs, and side-questing, while experience and point edits can be used to test different character builds.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate health or god mode before entering difficult encounters. Infinite SP supports repeated skill use, while damage and one-hit-kill options shorten battles. For a more natural experience, use only resource protection and leave damage settings disabled during bosses.
